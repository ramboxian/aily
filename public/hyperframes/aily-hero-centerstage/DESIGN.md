## Style Prompt

Build a transparent center-stage hero illustration for Feishu aily. The main action must always happen around the center of the illustration: Task appears in the center, the user click happens in the center, Team Lead takes over in the center, Agent members bloom outward from the center, and the final result returns to the center. The illustration is embedded directly into the homepage background, so do not draw a separate framed canvas or large container.

## Colors

- Text: `#172033`
- Muted text: `#738198`
- Primary blue: `#5B7CFF`
- Cyan accent: `#65D8CF`
- Violet accent: `#8A7CFF`
- White glass: `rgba(255,255,255,0.72)`
- Fine line: `rgba(91,124,255,0.22)`

## Typography

- Use generic sans-serif for stable capture.
- Keep text tiny and sparse: `发现 Task`, `运行中`, `Team Lead`, `文档`, `日程`, `项目`, `本地`, `Task 已完成`.
- Use skeleton marks and icons instead of business text.

## Motion

- The center is the visual anchor.
- Every major scene starts at the center.
- Agents expand from center to a balanced orbit, then their outputs return to center.
- Lines only appear during dispatch and merge moments.
- Use staged ease curves and hold readable frames.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not start the story from the top-left corner.
- Do not place the final result in the bottom-right corner.
- Do not create a big rounded rectangle or obvious image container.
- Do not keep old-scene elements visible when the next scene takes focus.
- Do not make the design look like a left-to-right flowchart.
